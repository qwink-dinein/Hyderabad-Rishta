## Why we built it

In Hyderabad, many families still search for a rishta through WhatsApp groups, forwarded biodata images and word of mouth. It works, but it is hard to compare profiles, and phone numbers and photos travel much further than anyone intended. Hyderabad Rishta brings the same search into one structured, private place.

## What we believe

- **Dignity:** a biodata should present a person respectfully and completely, not as a forwarded image.
- **Privacy:** your phone number is never shown on your profile, and you decide who sees your photos.
- **Consent:** contact details are shared only when both sides agree.
- **Simplicity:** parents and grandparents should be able to use it, in English, Urdu or Telugu.

## What Hyderabad Rishta is — and is not

Hyderabad Rishta is a platform for introductions. Families and individuals create a biodata, discover profiles and send interest requests. We do not arrange marriages, we do not verify identities, and we do not guarantee outcomes. We do act on reports, and members can block or report any profile.

## Where we are

We are starting with Hyderabad and Telangana, and Hyderabadi families living elsewhere in India and abroad are welcome. The app is available on Android now (a direct download on this website), with Google Play and the App Store coming soon.

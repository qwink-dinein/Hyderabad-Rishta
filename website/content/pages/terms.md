## Eligibility

You must be of legal marriageable age in your country (in India: 18 for women, 21 for men). Profiles created for relatives require their knowledge and consent.

## Honest information

Do not post false, misleading or someone else’s information. Do not request money. We may pause or remove profiles that break these rules.

## Payments

Paid features are one-time purchases with no automatic renewal. Prices shown before payment apply to that order.

## Safety

Meet in public places and involve family. Report suspicious behaviour from any profile. See our [safety guidance](/safety/).

## Liability

We provide a platform for introductions and do not verify identities or guarantee outcomes.

## Start with an honest, complete biodata

Families of brides read a groom’s biodata carefully: education and work, family background, where the couple will live and religious practice. Fill every section honestly. If your son is previously married, say so up front.

## Be realistic about preferences

Decide what truly matters — practice, education level, age range, city — and stay open on the rest. A preference list that is too narrow can hide excellent matches.

## Search where Hyderabadi families are

Ask relatives and the community, and use a dedicated platform. On Hyderabad Rishta you can see matching brides by age and area before you even sign up, then filter by education, profession and mother tongue.

## Approach with respect

Many families of brides are cautious, and rightly so. Write a short introduction from the family, mention who you are, and do not ask for photos or numbers in the first message. Let them decide when to share.

## Respect privacy

Brides’ families often keep photos visible only to families they approve. Accept that as a sign of a careful family, not a refusal.

## Next steps

Move to a family call, then a meeting with elders present. Confirm details independently and never send or accept money. Read [what families look for in a proposal](/blog/what-families-should-look-for-in-a-marriage-proposal/).

## What Hyderabad Rishta does

- **No phone numbers on profiles.** A number is shared only when its owner offers it and the other person accepts.
- **Photo control.** Photos can be visible to everyone, only to people you approve, or hidden.
- **Block and report.** Any member can block a profile (it disappears for them) or report it to our team.
- **Pause or delete.** Hide your profile any time, or delete your account from Settings.

## What we do not do

Hyderabad Rishta **does not verify identities**, education, employment or family details. Treat every profile as unverified until your family has confirmed the details in person.

## Practical safety steps

1. **Involve family early.** Let elders talk to elders.
2. **Share your number only when comfortable,** and never your home address at the start.
3. **Never send money** — not for "visa fees", "medical emergencies", "registration" or anything else. Report anyone who asks.
4. **Video call before meeting,** then meet in a public place with family.
5. **Check details independently** — workplace, education, family references.
6. **Trust your instincts.** If something feels wrong, stop and report.

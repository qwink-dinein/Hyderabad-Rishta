## What a Muslim marriage biodata should contain

| Section | What to include | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Basic details | Full name, date of birth, height, marital status, mother tongue | Write the date of birth, not just age |
| About | A few honest lines about personality, values and plans | Specific beats generic ("enjoys cooking for family" > "simple and caring") |
| Education & work | Highest education, profession, where you work | Income is optional — you can keep it private |
| Religious practice | Maslak / school of thought, prayer, fasting, Hajj/Umrah if relevant | Describe practice honestly; families value clarity |
| Family | Father’s and mother’s occupation, brothers and sisters (and how many are married), family type, where the family lives | No names or phone numbers of family members |
| Location | City, area, native place, willingness to relocate | NRIs: mention country and visa/plans |
| Partner preferences | Age range, education, location, practice, marital status | Keep it reasonable — too narrow hides good matches |
| Photos | One clear recent photo is enough to start | Choose who can see it |

## What NOT to put on a biodata

- Your **phone number or home address** — share them only after you trust the other family.
- **Family members’ names or contact details.**
- Anything you would not be comfortable seeing forwarded.

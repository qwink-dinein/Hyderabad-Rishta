## Cluster C — Free rishta (owner request 2026-11-02)

The owner asked for "free rights", "free rights group", "free rights hyderabad", "free rights muslim". These are almost certainly misspellings of **"free rishta"** (rishta → "rights"). ## Internal linking rules

- Homepage → /muslim-matrimony-hyderabad/, /how-it-works/, /muslim-matrimony-india/, /safety/, /privacy/, comparison guide.
- Every landing page → /how-it-works/ and 2–3 relevant guides; every guide → its landing page and 2–3 related guides (front matter `related`).
- Biodata guides → /muslim-marriage-biodata/ → "Create your biodata" (app).
- Privacy/safety guides → /privacy/, /safety/.
- Anchor text: descriptive and varied ("how Hyderabad Rishta works", "our biodata guide"); avoid repeating exact-match anchors.
- Breadcrumbs on every page except home (HTML + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD).

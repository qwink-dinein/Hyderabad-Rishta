## Ladki ka rishta, ladke ka rishta — one simple place

Many Hyderabadi families search for a rishta through relatives, community groups and WhatsApp. Hyderabad Rishta brings those proposals into one place, where every profile follows the same biodata format and every contact is shared only with consent.

## Finding a rishta for your daughter

1. Create her biodata — the app words every question for her ("What is her date of birth?").
2. Choose what is public. Many families keep photos visible only to families they approve.
3. Look at grooms that match your preferences, and wait for interests from families.
4. Share your number only when you are comfortable.

## Finding a rishta for your son

1. Create his biodata with education, profession and family details families ask about.
2. Discover brides by age, area, education and mother tongue.
3. Send a short, respectful introduction from the family.

## What families ask first

Age, height, education, work, family background, where they live, maslak and religious practice, and marital status. A complete biodata answers these up front. Use our [Muslim marriage biodata guide](/muslim-marriage-biodata/).

## Nikah ke liye rishta, with privacy

- No phone number on any profile.
- Photos only for the families you allow.
- Block or report any profile at any time.

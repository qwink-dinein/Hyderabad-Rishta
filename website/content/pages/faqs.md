## About Hyderabad Rishta

### What is Hyderabad Rishta?

Hyderabad Rishta is a Muslim matrimonial platform focused on helping individuals and families discover marriage profiles and meaningful rishta connections, starting with Hyderabad, India. It is available as an Android app, in English, Urdu and Telugu.

### Who can use it?

Muslim men and women of legal marriageable age (in India, 18 for women and 21 for men), and their families. A parent, brother, sister or relative can create a biodata for someone, with that person’s knowledge and consent.

### Is it only for Hyderabad?

We are starting with Hyderabad and Telangana. Hyderabadi and Indian Muslim families living elsewhere in India or abroad are welcome too.

## How it works

### How do I find a Muslim rishta in Hyderabad?

Create a biodata, choose the age range and area you are looking for, browse matching profiles, and send an interest to families you like. See [how it works](/how-it-works/) and our guide on [finding a Muslim rishta in Hyderabad](/blog/how-to-find-a-muslim-rishta-in-hyderabad/).

### How do I create a Muslim marriage biodata online?

Open the Hyderabad Rishta app, verify your mobile number with a one-time code on WhatsApp, and answer a few short questions — one per screen. Add photos, family details and preferences when you are ready. See the [biodata guide](/muslim-marriage-biodata/).

### What should a Muslim marriage biodata contain?

Basic details (name, date of birth, height, marital status, mother tongue), education and work, religious practice, family background, location and partner preferences. Leave out phone numbers, addresses and family members’ contact details.

### How can families approach a marriage proposal online?

Send a short, respectful introduction from the family, mention who you are writing for, and let the other family reply in their own time. Move to a phone call and a family meeting once both sides are comfortable.

## Privacy and safety

### Who can see my phone number?

You decide. In Privacy settings, choose **Everyone** (all signed-in members can see it on your profile) or **Only people I consent to** (it stays hidden until you offer it to a specific person and they accept). New grooms start with Everyone and new brides start private; you can change this at any time.

### Who can see my photos?

You choose: everyone who can see your biodata, only people you approve, or no one.

### What should I consider before sharing my contact details?

Read the full biodata, check that the family’s details are consistent, involve your own family, and consider a call before sharing a home address. Never send money. See our [safety page](/safety/).

### Are profiles verified?

No. Hyderabad Rishta does not verify identities, education or employment. Confirm details with the family before any commitment.

### Can I pause or delete my profile?

Yes. Pause it from your profile, or delete your account permanently from Settings. A copy is kept for 30 days for safety checks, visible only to our team, then erased.

## Compared with other options

### What are the alternatives to WhatsApp rishta groups?

Family and community networks, local marriage bureaus, and dedicated Muslim matrimonial apps like Hyderabad Rishta. Apps give you structured biodata, search filters and privacy controls that group chats do not. Read [WhatsApp rishta groups vs matrimonial apps](/blog/whatsapp-rishta-groups-vs-matrimonial-apps/).

### Is Hyderabad Rishta a marriage bureau?

No. It is a platform where families find and contact each other directly. There is no agent in between, and no fee to contact someone.

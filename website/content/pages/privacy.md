## Who we are

Hyderabad Rishta is a Muslim matrimonial service for adults and their families, run from Hyderabad, India. We decide why and how your personal data is used for the service, and we are responsible for it. Contact: {{EMAIL}}.

## Who may use it

Only adults of legal marriageable age: in India, 18 or older for a bride and 21 or older for a groom. The app checks the date of birth before a biodata can be created. Do not create a biodata for anyone under these ages. If we have reason to believe a biodata belongs to someone underage, we will hide and remove it. You can report this using Report on the profile or by email.

## What we collect

Account: your mobile number and country code, login records (one-time code checks, device sessions) and your PIN, which is stored only as a one-way scrambled code (a hash) and is never readable by anyone, including our team.

Biodata: what you enter, such as name, date of birth, height, marital status and children, mother tongue, city, education, profession, family details, religious practice and maslak, partner preferences, an "about me" note and optional photos. Religious, marital and family details and photos are especially personal; most fields are optional, and you choose who sees them.

Activity: interests and their messages, number offers, photo-sharing choices, shortlists, blocks, reports, profile views, notifications and Boost orders.

Technical: app version, device type, an app notification token (if you allow notifications) and security logs.

## Why we use it

To create and secure your account; to show your biodata to other members according to your settings; to run discovery, filters, shortlists, interests and consent-based sharing; to send you service notifications; to run Boost; to prevent fraud, harassment, impersonation and misuse and to handle reports; to keep the service working and improve it using counts that contain no names, numbers or biodata text; and to meet legal obligations.

We use your data with your consent (given when you create an account, publish your biodata or choose to share something) and, where the law allows, for legitimate uses such as security and legal compliance. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your rights"); some features will stop working without the data they need.

## Who can see your biodata

Your biodata is shown only to logged-in members, and only after you publish it. You can pause it at any time. Your mobile number is never shown on your profile. Your name is shown as you choose: your full name, first name or initials. Photos and family details can be set so that they are visible to members, or only to people you choose to share them with. Income is hidden unless you choose otherwise.

Biodata is never published on our website, never sent to search engines and never put on public pages. Our website only has general information.

## Photos

Photos are kept in private storage. They are shown through short-lived links, and only to people your settings allow. Before upload the app resizes your photo and removes hidden details stored inside the file, including GPS location. You can remove a photo or turn photo sharing off at any time. Once someone has seen a photo, we cannot stop them from having taken a screenshot.

## Your phone number

Your number is private by default. Sending or accepting an interest never shares a number by itself. A number is shared only when its owner taps to share it with one specific person, and the app asks for that consent each time. If you offer your number with an interest, the other person sees it only if they accept. If both of you share, both numbers show. You can withdraw an offer the other person has not yet accepted. Once someone has seen your number, we cannot take it back from them.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not show advertising. We use these service providers, only for what is listed:

- Supabase (database, login, photo storage and server functions). Data is stored in their Mumbai, India region.
- Waplify, to deliver your one-time login code on WhatsApp (your mobile number and the code only).
- Expo, Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple, to deliver push notifications if you allow them (a device token and a short message with no biodata).
- Google Play and the Apple App Store, to distribute the app.
- A licensed payment provider, when paid Boost is switched on (order amount and status; your card or UPI details stay with the provider).

We may also disclose data when Indian law, a court order or a valid government request requires it, or when it is needed to protect someone's safety.

## Data outside India

Your biodata, photos and account data are stored in India (Mumbai). Push notification and messaging services may process the small amount of data they need (a device token, your number for the login code) outside India. We follow any restrictions the Government of India sets on such transfers.

## How long we keep it

- Your biodata and photos: while your account is active.
- After you delete your account, it disappears from the app at once. A copy of the biodata and photos is kept for 30 days, visible only to the Hyderabad Rishta team, for safety, fraud and complaint checks. It is then erased automatically.
- Database backups expire within 30 days.
- Notifications: your newest 200 are kept; read ones older than 180 days are deleted.
- Push delivery records: 30 days. Account-recovery attempts: 30 days. Boost view counts: 180 days. Usage counts (with no names, numbers or text): 400 days.
- An unanswered interest expires after 14 days.
- Login codes stop working after 10 minutes.
- Security and audit records, consent records, payment and tax records, and reports: as long as the law requires or a dispute needs.

## Your rights

You can:
- Correct or update your biodata at any time in the app.
- Withdraw consent: turn photo sharing off, withdraw a number offer, pause your biodata, or turn notifications off.
- Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
- Ask for a summary of the data we hold about you, and who we shared it with, by email.
- Nominate, by email, another person to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself.
- Complain to our Grievance Officer (below), and, if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

We will check that a request really comes from the account owner. Write from the account's mobile number or tell us the number. We will never ask for your PIN or login code.

## Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Data is encrypted at rest by our host. Every member can reach only their own data, enforced on the server. Team accounts are named (no shared logins) and protected by a PIN, and admin actions are recorded in an audit log. Full phone numbers, PINs, login codes and biodata are kept out of our logs and usage counts. No online service can promise perfect security. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Grievance Officer

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact the Grievance Officer of Hyderabad Rishta: {{EMAIL}}. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

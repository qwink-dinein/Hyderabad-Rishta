## Step 1: Agree on what you are looking for

Before searching, sit together as a family — including the person getting married — and agree on the basics: age range, education, where the couple will live, maslak and level of practice, and anything that is genuinely non-negotiable. Write it down. Families who agree early waste far less time on proposals that were never going to work.

## Step 2: Prepare a complete, honest biodata

Most first impressions in a rishta search come from the biodata. Include date of birth, height, education, profession, family background, mother tongue, religious practice and partner preferences. Leave out phone numbers and home addresses. Use our [biodata format in English](/blog/muslim-marriage-biodata-format-english/) as a starting point.

## Step 3: Use the channels that suit your family

Hyderabadi families usually combine several of these:

- **Relatives and community elders** — still the most trusted route for many families.
- **Local marriage bureaus** — helpful for some, but ask how they share your details.
- **WhatsApp rishta groups** — large reach, but your number and photos travel far. See [the trade-offs](/blog/whatsapp-rishta-groups-vs-matrimonial-apps/).
- **Muslim matrimonial apps** — structured profiles, filters and privacy controls. [Hyderabad Rishta](/muslim-matrimony-hyderabad/) is built for Hyderabadi families.

## Step 4: Shortlist with care

Read each biodata fully. Look for consistency (age, education and work that fit together) and for what matters to your family. Shortlist a few strong matches rather than contacting dozens at once.

## Step 5: Approach the family respectfully

Send a short introduction that says who you are and who you are writing for — for example, "Assalamu alaikum, we are looking for a rishta for our son, an engineer working in Hyderabad. We liked your daughter’s biodata and would be happy to share more about our family." Give the other family time to reply. Read more on [approaching a proposal](/faqs/#how-can-families-approach-a-marriage-proposal-online).

## Step 6: Talk, verify, then meet

Move to a phone or video call between families, ask questions openly, and confirm education, work and family details independently. Meet in a public place or at home with elders present. Never send money to anyone you met online.

## Step 7: Give it time

A good rishta search often takes months. Keep your biodata up to date, pause it when you are in talks with a family, and resume when you are ready.

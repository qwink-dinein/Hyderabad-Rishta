## Protect her privacy from day one

Choose a biodata platform where you control who sees photos and contact details. On Hyderabad Rishta, photos can be visible only to families you approve, the phone number is never on the profile, and her name can be shown as initials.

## Write a biodata that answers families’ questions

Education, work or study plans, family background, religious practice and preferences. Be clear about plans after marriage — continuing work or study, staying in Hyderabad or relocating.

## Look beyond the biodata

For a groom, families often look at stability (work, plans, responsibilities at home), character and family values. Ask people who know him, and confirm his workplace and education yourself.

## Let interests come to you — and send your own

You can wait for families to send interest, and you can also send interests to grooms you like. Both are normal and respectable.

## Share contact only when comfortable

Accept an interest, talk family to family, and offer your number only when you are ready. Never share your home address early, and never send money.

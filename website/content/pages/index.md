## Who Hyderabad Rishta is for

Hyderabad Rishta is made for **Muslim families and individuals looking for marriage**: parents searching for a rishta for their son or daughter, brothers and sisters helping a sibling, and young men and women creating their own biodata. If you have been relying on WhatsApp rishta groups or a local marriage bureau, it gives you the same goal — finding a suitable match — with structure and privacy.

## How it works

<ol class="steps">
<li><strong>1. Tell us who it’s for</strong>Yourself, your son, your daughter, a brother, a sister or a relative. A few taps, no typing.</li>
<li><strong>2. See matching profiles first</strong>Before you give any number, see how many brides or grooms match the age and area you want.</li>
<li><strong>3. Create the biodata</strong>Verify your mobile number with a one-time code on WhatsApp, then answer a few short questions — one per screen.</li>
<li><strong>4. Connect with consent</strong>Send an interest. Numbers and private details are shared only when both sides agree.</li>
</ol>

Read the full guide: [how Hyderabad Rishta works](/how-it-works/).

## What you can do

<ul class="features">
<li><strong>Structured biodata</strong>Basics, education and work, family, religious practice and partner preferences — in one clear format instead of scattered WhatsApp messages.</li>
<li><strong>Discover profiles</strong>Browse Muslim bride and groom profiles and filter by age, city, education, profession, marital status and mother tongue.</li>
<li><strong>Interest requests</strong>Send an introduction or offer your number. The other family decides whether to accept.</li>
<li><strong>Consent-based contact</strong>Phone numbers are never shown on profiles. They are shared only when you offer and the other side accepts.</li>
<li><strong>Privacy controls</strong>Choose who sees your photos, income and family details, and how your name appears.</li>
<li><strong>Shortlist & Boost</strong>Save profiles you like. Optionally boost your profile for extra visibility (a paid, clearly labelled feature).</li>
</ul>

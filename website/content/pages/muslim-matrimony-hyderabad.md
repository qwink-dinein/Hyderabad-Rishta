## Muslim matrimony, made for how Hyderabad searches

Rishta searches in Hyderabad have always been a family affair: parents, elders, relatives and community groups all help. Hyderabad Rishta keeps that family-first approach and adds what WhatsApp groups and paper biodata cannot: a structured biodata, filters to find suitable matches, and privacy controls so your number and photos are not passed around.

## What you will find

- **Muslim bride and groom profiles** in a consistent biodata format: age, height, education, profession, family background, religious practice, mother tongue and partner preferences.
- **Filters that matter to families:** age range, area, education, profession, marital status and mother tongue (Urdu, Dakhini, Telugu, Hindi, English and more).
- **Profiles created by families:** a biodata can be made by the person themselves or by a parent, brother, sister or relative — the profile says who created it.

## Areas across Hyderabad

Families on Hyderabad Rishta come from neighbourhoods across the city and nearby — Old City, Charminar, Malakpet, Chandrayangutta, Mehdipatnam, Tolichowki, Shaikpet, Attapur, Nampally and Secunderabad — and from Hyderabadi families living in other cities and abroad. You choose which area to search in.

## Privacy first

- Your phone number is **never** shown on your profile.
- Photos can be visible to everyone, only to people you approve, or hidden.
- Contact details are shared only when you offer and the other family accepts.

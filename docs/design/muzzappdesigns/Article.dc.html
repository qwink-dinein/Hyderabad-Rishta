How to create an effective marriage biodata

A clear biodata helps families decide faster — and keeps your private details private. Here is what to include, in what order.

On this page

Families read many biodatas in a week. The ones that get a reply are easy to scan: the essentials first, in the same order every time, with nothing that could put the family at risk.

Start with the basics

Name as you want it shown, age, height, marital status, mother tongue and the city you live in. One line each — no paragraphs.

  • Use your locality, never your full address.
  • Say who is creating the biodata: the person or a family member.

Education and work

Your highest qualification, what you do and where. Income is optional — on Hyderabad Rishta you choose whether it is shown.

Family details

Parents’ occupations, siblings and family type help families picture the household. Keep it short and factual.

What to keep private

Never put your phone number, home address or ID numbers in a biodata. Share contact details only after both families agree.

## 1. Keep your phone number off the biodata

A number on a biodata is shared with everyone who ever sees it. Share it with one family at a time, after a first conversation. On Hyderabad Rishta, numbers are never shown on profiles — they are exchanged only when both sides agree.

## 2. Decide who sees photos

Photos are the details that get forwarded most. Choose a platform that lets you show photos only to families you approve, and use one clear, simple photo rather than many.

## 3. Show only as much of the name as you need

First name or initials are enough for a first look. Share the full name once families are talking.

## 4. Leave family members’ details out

Never list parents’ or siblings’ names, workplaces or phone numbers on a biodata. Describe them ("father: retired government employee") instead.

## 5. Watch for money requests

No genuine family asks for money, gift cards, "visa fees" or "medical help" during a rishta search. Stop, block and report.

## 6. Use block and report

If someone makes you uncomfortable, block them. Report fake profiles or harassment so the platform can act.

## 7. Pause when you are in talks

When you are seriously talking to one family, pause your profile. It keeps things respectful and your details out of circulation.

## 8. Know how deletion works

Before signing up anywhere, check what happens to your data when you leave. On Hyderabad Rishta, deleting your account removes it from the app at once; a copy is kept for 30 days for safety checks, visible only to the team, then erased. Read the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

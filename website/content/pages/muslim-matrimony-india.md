## For Indian Muslim families outside Hyderabad

Families in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi and elsewhere often still prefer a match with Hyderabadi roots, or simply want a dedicated Muslim matrimonial platform. You can choose the country, state and city when you search, and see profiles from anywhere if you prefer.

## For NRI Muslim families

Many Hyderabadi families in the Gulf, the UK, the USA, Canada and Australia look for a rishta from home. On Hyderabad Rishta:

- Choose your country of residence in your biodata, and whether you are willing to relocate.
- Search profiles in Hyderabad, elsewhere in India, or abroad.
- Sign up with your own country’s mobile number.

## Educated and professional profiles

Every biodata includes highest education and profession, so families looking for an educated bride or groom — engineers, doctors, teachers, business families — can filter for what matters to them. Hyderabad Rishta does not verify education or employment; ask the family for details as the conversation progresses.

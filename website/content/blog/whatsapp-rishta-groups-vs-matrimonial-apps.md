## What free rishta groups do well

- **Free and familiar.** Everyone already uses WhatsApp.
- **Community trust.** Groups run by people you know feel safer than strangers.
- **Reach.** A biodata posted in a few groups is seen by many families quickly.

## Where they fall short

- **Your phone number is visible** to everyone in the group, and to anyone a message is forwarded to.
- **Photos travel without control.** A biodata image can be forwarded far beyond the group.
- **Hard to compare.** Every biodata is a different image or long message.
- **No search.** You scroll through hundreds of posts hoping the right one appears.
- **Old posts stay around** long after a rishta is fixed.

## Marriage bureaus

Bureaus offer personal attention and local knowledge. Ask how they store and share your biodata, what they charge and whether they share your number without asking.

## Using them together

Many families use more than one. A practical approach: keep your community connections, and put your detailed biodata where you control who sees your number and photos.

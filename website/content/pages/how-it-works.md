## 1. Tell us who the biodata is for

Choose yourself, your son, your daughter, a brother, a sister or a relative. If you are making it for yourself or a relative, you also choose whether you are looking for a bride or a groom. Everything after that is worded for the right person — "his name", "her age".

## 2. See matching profiles before you sign up

Pick an age range and where you live. Before asking for any phone number, the app shows how many brides or grooms match, with a few safe details (first letter of the name, age, profession and city). No names, photos or numbers are shown at this stage.

## 3. Verify your mobile number

Enter your mobile number and the one-time code we send on WhatsApp. Then choose a 4-digit PIN for quick logins later. One mobile number holds one biodata.

## 4. Answer a few short questions

One question per screen: name, date of birth, height, marital status (and children, only if married before), mother tongue, city, education and profession. Every answer is saved immediately — you can stop and continue later.

## 5. Complete the rest when you are ready

Add up to three photos, family background, religious practice and partner preferences. A profile with more detail is easier for families to understand. See our [biodata guide](/muslim-marriage-biodata/).

## 6. Discover and shortlist

Browse profiles and filter by age, city, education, profession, marital status and mother tongue. Save the ones you like to your shortlist.

## 7. Send an interest — with consent

Open a profile and choose how to connect: send a short introduction, or offer your phone number. The other family accepts or declines. If they don’t reply, you can send one gentle reminder after a while.

## 8. Share contact details only when both agree

Phone numbers are never shown on profiles. A number is revealed only when its owner offers it and the other person accepts. If you have both offered numbers, they are exchanged straight away.

## Your privacy controls

- **Name:** show your full name, first name only, or initials.
- **Photos:** visible to everyone, only to people you approve, or hidden.
- **Income and family details:** public, on approval, or hidden.
- **Pause, block, report or delete** at any time. Read the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

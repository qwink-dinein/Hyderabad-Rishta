> This policy is being reviewed by a lawyer for India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If anything here and the app ever differ, contact us and we will correct it.

## What we collect

Your mobile number (for login), the biodata you choose to enter, optional photos, your partner preferences, and activity needed to run the service (requests, shortlists, profile views, notifications).

## What we do not do

We never show your mobile number on your profile. We do not sell personal data. We do not compute compatibility scores from religious practice.

## Who can see your data

Published biodata is visible to logged-in members according to your privacy settings. Photos, income and family details can be limited to people you explicitly consent to. Contact details are shared only after you accept a request and separately consent.

Member profiles are **not** published on this website or in search engines. This website contains only general information about Hyderabad Rishta.

## Your choices

You can edit or pause your profile, change visibility, block or report members, deactivate your account, or permanently delete it and its data from Settings.

## Retention

When you delete your account it disappears from the app immediately and no member can see it again. For safety, fraud and complaint checks, a copy of your biodata and photos is kept for 30 days where only the Hyderabad Rishta team can see it, then erased automatically. Backups expire within 30 days. Consent, payment and audit records may be kept as long as legally required.

## This website

This website does not set cookies and does not ask for personal information. If we turn on website statistics, we use a cookie-free service that counts visits without identifying you.

## Contact

For privacy questions or to request your data, see our [contact page](/contact/).
